## Art pass (2026-11-19, captures `a1`..`a5`; the user asked for a deeper pass on the weakest areas)

What changed:
- **Cockpit** rebuilt and rebaked at 4K (16 + 27 min, slowed by parallel jobs): staggered keyboard with a legend on
  every cap and accent keys, stencilled labels as geometry, bolts on ribs, lockers and the window frame, switch
  rows, richer wall displays, lamp louvres, more star light and less lamp light. The display housing is its own
  node and is narrowed to 56% on phones, so both real bezels are in frame at reading size.
- **Home terrain** reworked by Codex (`gpt-6.2-sol`, workspace-write, limited to `tools/blender/build_terrain.py`
  and its outputs; I reviewed its preview, report and file list): six distinct formations with scree, a crater
  rim built into the height function, lander pad moved 1.6 m right so the lander stands against dark sky.
  Triangles 262,242 -> 291,830; `home_terrain.glb` 1.74 MB compressed.
- **Planets and star**: gas giant with uneven jets, belts, eddies and three storms; craters with individual
  depth, age and outline; lava world as crust plates with a few molten seams; cool fill on night sides; star
  surface as bright granules with thin dark lanes; corona held closer to the limb.
- **System view**: opaque label plates, leaders that end on the planet with a dot, phone number badges on the
  limb, a faint dust glow in the orbital plane, a crescent rim on the home planet.
- **Phone project view**: full entries keep the upright display with the dish moved to its top edge and the
  picture first; sparse entries get a small landscape display under a large planet.
- **Transitions**: the open hatch is lit (it had been black since the first build: wrong layer order and a black
  exported emissive colour); leaving the cockpit comes out in front of the lit hatch with ladder and horizon in
  frame; the launch camera tracks the lander on a long lens; flight starts beside the lit ringed planet with
  map graphics dimmed.
- **Screens**: a heading is never left alone at the fold; a link-less project no longer shows a scroll cue it
  caused itself.

Regression found and fixed inside this pass:
- The richer planet shader froze the page for about 35 s after the menu appeared (98% of main-thread time in
  shader linking; the phone journey timed out on its first tap). Fixed by one small program per planet type,
  8-cell crater loops, and compiling every shader (including hidden objects) behind the loading screen against
  the same kind of buffer the scene is drawn into. `node tools/qa/shader-links.mjs` afterwards: no link over
  60 ms after the loader lifts; three blocking links remain during loading (environment filter, shadow depth,
  one post-processing pass; 0.6-2.2 s each on battery).

How the earlier "41 fps" and "46 fps" readings came about, for the record:
- First run after the pass, on mains: home 41 fps, project 48-52, others 55-60. The desktop app's own preview
  pane was rendering the site and using about half the GPU.
- Runs on battery: every view 45-47 fps with the same 23-24 ms cadence whatever is drawn (battery pacing).
- Two real faults were found while chasing this and are fixed: (1) my first change to the resolution scaler
  ("fall back to the lowest size when it keeps flipping") was triggered on fast machines too, because the
  library counts every upward step as a flip, so cheap views were being drawn at 1344x756 for nothing; (2) a
  first rewrite mistook a view locked at half the refresh rate (even 33 ms frames) for a battery-paced browser
  and left home at 39 fps. The scaler is now a small deterministic one: start sharp, step down only on missed
  refreshes measured against the fastest interval the device has shown, never retry a size that failed.
- Cost reductions made along the way and kept: MSAA capped at 2x (was 4x at 1x pixel density), bloom at half
  resolution, three noise lookups removed from the star and corona and five from the gas giant, the dust disc
  not drawn from the surface.

Re-run on the final code after the scaler change: type-check clean, build OK (7.0 MB), 37 captures with 0
console errors, journeys 20/20 on laptop, phone and reduced motion, hidden extras 17/17, studio 15/15, and
`shader-links.mjs --desktop` shows no slow link after the loader lifts (during loading: 0.5-2.1 s each for the
